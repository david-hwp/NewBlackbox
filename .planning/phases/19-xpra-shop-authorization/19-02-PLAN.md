  <files>
    admin/scripts/browser/zr-login-align.js
  </files>
  <action>

Stop auto-alignment after user interaction.

    - The transform preserves pointer hit testing.
    - Alignment failure falls back to full page display without blocking the stream.
  </done>
</task>
